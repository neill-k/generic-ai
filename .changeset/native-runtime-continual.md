Add the continual harness (`@generic-ai/continual`), a port of Prime Agent's `/refine`. Native sessions with `continual` keep supplemental prompt notes, memories, skills and subagent specs in a global and a session-local store, show them in the system prompt ranked by relevance, and refine them from evidence with the `refine` tool, `/refine` prompts and an automatic review, with history and rollback.
